Preview v0.1.0 · macOS · Apple Silicon

Hold Fn, speak, and it types.

Voice typing for Hindi, English and Hinglish. Hinglish comes out in Roman letters ("kal ki meeting postpone kar do").

Everything runs on your Mac. It works offline, and your voice never leaves the computer.

Dictation

Most accurate

  • Large Hinglish speech model (Whisper large-v3-turbo, fine-tuned)
  • Text in about 0.3–0.6 s after you let go of Fn
  • About 0.7 GB of memory while dictating; it unloads after 5 minutes idle

Dictation Lite

Small and fastest

  • Small model (Whisper base size), distilled for Indian speech
  • Text in about 0.2 s after you let go of Fn
  • About 130 MB of memory while dictating; it unloads after 5 minutes idle

Needs a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 11 or later. Run one version at a time: both listen for the Fn key.

Install

  1. Double-click the download to unzip it in Finder, then drag the app into your Applications folder.
  2. Open it. This preview is not notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks it the first time. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
  3. When asked, allow Accessibility (to hear the Fn key and type for you) and Microphone.
  4. If Fn opens the emoji picker, set System Settings → Keyboard → "Press 🌐 key to" to Do Nothing.

The app lives in the menu bar (a small dot); quit it from there.

Privacy

Speech recognition runs on your Mac. The app makes no network connections for dictation, keeps no recordings by default, and has no account or analytics.

Known limits of this preview

  • macOS only for now. Windows, Android and iOS are planned.
  • Not signed with an Apple developer certificate, so the first launch needs "Open Anyway".
  • Dictation Lite is less accurate on English than Dictation.
