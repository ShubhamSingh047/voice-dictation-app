Preview v0.2.2 · macOS · Apple Silicon

Bolkey

Bolo. Likh gaya.

Hold Fn, speak Hindi, English or Hinglish, and it types where your cursor is. Hinglish comes out in Roman letters ("kal ki meeting postpone kar do").

Everything runs on your Mac. It works offline, and your voice never leaves the computer.

Bolkey

Most accurate · recommended · blue

  • Large Hinglish speech model (Whisper large-v3-turbo, fine-tuned)
  • Text in about 0.3–0.6 s after you let go of Fn
  • About 0.7 GB of memory while the model is loaded; it frees itself after 5 minutes idle

Needs a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 11 or later. Run one version at a time: both listen for the Fn key. The pill on screen is tinted with the running version's colour: blue for Bolkey, orange for Bolkey Lite.

Install

  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag Bolkey onto the Applications folder.
  2. Open it. This preview is not notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks it the first time. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
  3. When asked, allow Accessibility (to hear the Fn key and type for you) and Microphone. Bolkey starts listening as soon as you allow it.
  4. If Fn opens the emoji picker, set System Settings → Keyboard → "Press 🌐 key to" to Do Nothing.

Bolkey lives in the menu bar (a small keycap icon). From there: language, pill position, how soon to free memory, launch at login, your notes, and Quit.

Privacy

Speech recognition runs on your Mac. Bolkey makes no network connections, keeps no recordings, and has no account or analytics.

Instant start (on by default) keeps the microphone open so the first word is never clipped: Bolkey holds the last quarter-second of sound in memory, overwritten continuously, and only uses it when you press Fn. macOS shows its microphone indicator while this is on. Turn it off in the menu bar ("Instant start") to open the mic only while you hold Fn.

Known limits of this preview

  • macOS only for now. Windows, Android and iOS are planned.
  • Not notarized by Apple, so the first launch needs "Open Anyway".
  • Bolkey Lite is noticeably less accurate on English than Bolkey; use Bolkey for English.
